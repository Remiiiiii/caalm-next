# South Florida top-ranking checklist

This is the CEO assessment of shipped CAALM, rewritten as a checklist.

Checked items are already true in the product we bought (compliance and agreement lifecycle). Unchecked items are the negatives rewritten as the outcome that must be true. When every box in **Top-ranking bar** is checked, CAALM meets the bar for a top-ranking South Florida nonprofit: clean site visits, money that does not lapse, a board that trusts the numbers, and a development shop that can compete with the big Miami and Broward names.

Judge the product as it ships today. The Nonprofit Roadmap is an engineering board until the boxes below are live for staff.

---

## How CAALM already changed the organization

Before CAALM, agreements lived in email, shared drives, and one spreadsheet someone owned. Renewals were calendar invites. When a funder or DCF asked who approved a grant and when it ends, we assembled a packet by hand.

- [x] Agreements, licenses, and dollar streams we already won live in one shared system of record
- [x] Program, finance, and the CEO stop arguing about which copy is current
- [x] The org is calmer about compliance (licenses, grants, expirations)
- [x] CAALM is honest: it is compliance and agreement lifecycle, not a donor CRM we pretend we have

---

## Features that already help (keep these sharp)

Each item is a shipped benefit. Treat unchecked follow-ons as the next standard for that same feature.

### Contracts as the spine

- [x] Grants, vendor deals, leases, MOUs, and donation agreements sit in one list with owner, dates, files, and an approval trail
- [x] When Miami-Dade or a foundation asks for the executed grant, staff pull the record, not a 2023 email thread
- [ ] Every live grant and license is loaded with an owner before we call the org live (onboarding is people work, not a license key)

### Approvals and e-sign

- [x] Drafts move through review instead of bouncing in Outlook
- [x] Signatures leave a trail: who signed, not who said they signed
- [x] The week between “board said yes” and “the county has the executed copy” is shorter

### Clause library and templates

- [x] Standard wording (insurance, indemnification, data-sharing, grant terms) lives in one place
- [x] Staff stop rewriting the same paragraph under pressure
- [x] A new development hire does not invent a new grant packet from scratch

### Funding & Retention (keep if we can keep only one)

- [x] Live contracts rank by dollars at stake and health: at risk, protecting, protected, expired
- [x] The work that keeps the money (reports, renewals, deliverables) attaches to the stream
- [x] The CEO can walk into a board finance committee with “this much is at risk, and here is the next action”
- [x] Pursuits (including SAM.gov notices) sit next to retention and become a proposal when we win
- [x] Retention is money we have; pursuits are money we do not; a live grant cannot hide inside a pipeline tab
- [ ] Staff are trained on retention vs pursuit before the first board packet from CAALM

### Licenses

- [x] DCF, AHCA, occupancy, and other credentials track like contracts: owner, expiry, files
- [x] A lapsed license shows on our calendar, not from a monitor’s visit

### Audit readiness

- [x] Weekly snapshots, a score, a punch list that jumps into the contract or license, and a PDF packet
- [x] The product states this is not a DCF, HRSA, or CPA opinion
- [x] Before a site visit we already know which files are missing (rehearsal, not a scramble)
- [x] Insights can tag HRSA OSV, child-welfare monitoring, and financial PBC lanes

### Calendar tied to the work

- [x] Deadlines sit next to the agreement they protect
- [x] Outlook can connect
- [ ] Managers live in the calendar so “I thought you had it” stays rare

### Permissions and roles

- [x] Viewers watch; department managers see their division; org admins run invites and billing
- [x] Access comes from assigned keys, not “allowed because someone is the CEO”
- [ ] Real jobs are mapped onto permission keys in week one (not everyone handed Admin)

### Assistant (AI), gated

- [x] Chat, document analysis, meeting prep, and audit-summary help exist
- [x] A 40-page grant can become a briefing before a call
- [x] AI stays on a short list of people and is not treated as a lawyer

### Tickets, news, files, org structure

- [x] Internal tickets, staff news, a document library, departments, and budget codes exist
- [x] Small fires stay out of all-staff email

## Top-ranking bar (negatives rewritten as the outcome)

These were the gaps. Each line is the positive that must be true. Checking every box here (plus the South Florida extras below) is the definition of done.

### Constituent and gift spine

- [ ] Donors, volunteers, and members live in CAALM as constituent records; we do not keep a second Bloomerang or spreadsheet as the person file
- [ ] Households and relationships are on the person, so a gift can soft-credit the right people
- [ ] Gifts post once, get a unique receipt number, and cannot be silently edited
- [ ] Designations map to fund codes
- [ ] Gifts can link to the grant contract they support
- [ ] Development and compliance meet in CAALM, not in a spreadsheet

### Restricted funds on the grant

- [ ] Every grant picks a fund (unrestricted, temporarily restricted, or permanently restricted)
- [ ] Purpose restrictions stay as narrative next to the coded fund
- [ ] Budget vs actual comes from obligations and gifts on that grant
- [ ] Finance can export a Form 990 *worksheet* (not e-file) for the CPA
- [ ] Two systems no longer lie to each other the week before the audit

### Explainable fundraising scores (no wealth scraping)

- [ ] Gift officers see RFM / lifecycle segment, lapse risk, upgrade readiness, and a suggested ask on the profile
- [ ] Each score lists the top reasons that moved the number
- [ ] We can import a wealth-screen CSV (iWave, DonorSearch) when we buy that screen
- [ ] CAALM never scrapes wealth data and never claims it is a wealth engine
- [ ] Local donors have no reason to call the product creepy

### Volunteers and public events that create constituents

- [ ] Volunteer shifts, hours, waivers, and grant-tagged hour letters live in CAALM
- [ ] Public events (gala, walk, hurricane volunteer shifts) have registration and QR check-in
- [ ] Check-in creates or matches a constituent
- [ ] A gift at registration posts as a gift on that person
- [ ] Program and development share one people file

### Stewardship that fires from posted gifts

- [ ] A posted gift sends one receipt (never two, never for anonymous or do-not-contact)
- [ ] A thank-you task is created from the posted gift
- [ ] An at-risk donor queue tells staff who to call
- [ ] Thank-you happens inside 48 hours; that is how we win the next gift in this market

### Spanish and Haitian Creole where we serve

- [ ] Internal CAALM staff UI can stay English
- [ ] The public give page, receipts, and preference center ship in English and Spanish
- [ ] Haitian Creole is available where we serve Creole-speaking households
- [ ] Miami-Dade and Broward donor UX is not English-only

### Hurricane / continuity pack

- [ ] One board lists grants with disaster clauses, licenses that lapse if a site is closed, and who to call
- [ ] The pack uses the contracts and licenses we already store; no second binder
- [ ] After a storm, leadership can answer funders and monitors from CAALM the same day

### Phone companion for the field

- [ ] Site managers can approve, attest, renew, and check a license on a phone
- [ ] Door staff can check in an event or volunteer shift on a phone
- [ ] Full wizards, libraries, and analytics stay laptop-only
- [ ] A classroom or site visit does not require a laptop to stay legal

### Funder-shaped visit modes

- [ ] Audit readiness still produces the rehearsal packet
- [ ] The same data prints as visit modes for Children’s Services Council, county human services, DCF, United Way, HRSA-style OSV, child-welfare monitoring, and PBC
- [ ] A grant funder snapshot shows restrictions, fund, budget vs actual, related gifts, and tagged volunteer hours
- [ ] The board pack is live YTD dollars, retention, and campaign ROI, not a reconstructed Excel

### Stay the system of record (do not become a messy second Salesforce)

- [x] CAALM does not replace MIP, Intacct, or the CPA
- [x] No general ledger posting, no SEFA, no payroll, no 990 e-file
- [ ] Journal / IIF export gives the accountant gifts and restriction releases
- [ ] Packaging and in-app help name those out-of-lane items and never claim them
- [ ] CAALM remains the record for agreements, licenses, restricted dollars, and people who give time or money

## Done when

All boxes in **Top-ranking bar** are checked.

Then a Miami board can open one login and see: the money is restricted correctly, the license is current, and the donor was thanked. That is top ranking here. Not a prettier PDF. A shop that never reconstructs the truth the week before the monitor arrives.

Until then, run shipped CAALM hard on contracts, licenses, and retention. That already stops the org that loses a grant to a missed report.
