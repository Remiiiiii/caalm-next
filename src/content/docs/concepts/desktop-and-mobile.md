CAALM is a **desktop-primary** product. The full workspace is built for a laptop or desktop (about 1024 pixels wide and up). Tablets use that same full app. Phones get a short companion set of actions, not a second product.

This matches how other operations platforms work: you approve or glance from a phone, and you create, analyze, and configure on a laptop.

## Why this split exists

Dense work in CAALM uses side-by-side panels, large tables, PDF/DOCX review, and multi-step wizards. Shrinking those screens onto a phone makes the work slower and easier to get wrong. So phones stay for interrupt work: approve, check a ticket, glance at your dashboard, then finish the rest on a laptop.

## What each device can do

| Device | What you get |
|---|---|
| Laptop / desktop (1024px and wider) | The full product: contracts, licenses, negotiation, analytics, calendar, audits, settings, IT portal |
| Tablet | Same full product as a laptop. Treat it as a smaller screen, not a phone app |
| Phone (under 1024px) | Companion only: home dashboard, contract and license approvals, tickets, team tasks, company news |

## Phone companion (works now)

On a phone you can:

- Open your **role dashboard** and expiry / approval widgets
- Work **Proposals & Approvals** for contracts and licenses (card lists with larger Approve/Review taps)
- Open **tickets** (list and detail) and **team tasks** (card lists + stacked filters)
- Read **company news** (feed first; stats stay on wider screens)
- Use notifications from the header while you are on a companion page

Expiry attestation and renew still open from the dashboard widgets on a phone. Links that need the contracts library or analytics ask you to finish on a laptop instead of sending you to a dead-end gate.

## Laptop-only (Open on a laptop)

If you open a dense page on a phone, CAALM shows **Open this page on a laptop** instead of a cramped layout. Copy the link and finish there.

Laptop-only includes:

- Contract and license libraries, upload wizards, templates, and negotiation
- Analytics and portfolio views
- Calendar
- Audits and audit readiness
- Settings, billing, permissions, and organization admin
- The IT portal (`/dashboard/it`) and user-management / role-admin tools
- Search and other wide tables

Public pages stay usable on a phone:

- Marketing / landing
- Docs (this site)
- Counterparty negotiation at `/negotiate/[token]`

## If you landed here from a phone

1. Copy the link from the **Open this page on a laptop** screen.
2. Open it on a desktop or laptop.
3. Or tap **Go to dashboard** and do the phone-safe work first.

A tablet at laptop width does not need this step. If your tablet is in a narrow portrait view and you see the gate, rotate it or open the same link on a laptop.
