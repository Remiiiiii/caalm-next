Deadlines only matter if a human notices them with enough time to act.

## Channels you may see

- **In-app Notification Center** (bell) — filters, mark read, deep links
- **Email** — operational notices and invite/auth messages
- **SMS** — used for certain admin/manager onboarding and auth completion flows when configured
- **Calendar** — visible time-based planning alongside records

## What typically triggers attention

- Upcoming contract / license expirations
- Approval requests and decisions
- Invites and security events (including 2FA-related flows)
- System/admin operational notices

## Make alerts useful

1. Keep expiration dates accurate (garbage dates ⇒ garbage alerts).
2. Tune personal notification settings under `/settings` so important categories stay loud.
3. Do not silence everything because one noisy type annoyed you — surgically mute.
4. Treat Notification Center as a work queue, not a museum.

## Failure mode to fear

The quiet miss: record exists, date is wrong or owner left, notifications go to nobody useful, leadership dashboard still looks “mostly fine” until it is not.

Fix path: [Missed renewal or quiet notifications](/docs/troubleshooting/missed-renewals).
