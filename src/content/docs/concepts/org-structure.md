CAALM is multi-tenant at the **organization** level. Inside an org, work is further scoped by departments/divisions so managers are not drowning in everyone else’s agreements.

## Organization

The hard boundary. Users, roles, billing, and records belong to an organization. You should never see another org’s contracts.

## Divisions (examples in product)

Behavioral health, child welfare, clinic, c-suite, CFS, HR, residential, support, help desk, accounting — and related mappings into parent departments such as IT, Finance, Administration, Legal, Operations.

Exact labels can evolve; the *idea* stays stable: **division is how department managers get a meaningful home**.

## What breaks when division is wrong

- Department Manager dashboards look empty or oddly global
- Analytics division pages disagree with “my contracts”
- Invites onboard people into the wrong operating context

## Admin checklist

1. Define the division list your org actually uses.
2. Set division on invite — do not “fix it later” for managers.
3. Re-check division when someone changes teams.
4. Prefer permission changes over silent cross-division visibility hacks.

## Practical analogy

Organization = company building. Division = floor. Permissions = which keys open which rooms on your floor.
