This is the shortest path to value. Follow the track that matches how you arrived.

## Path A — You were invited

1. Open the invite email and click **Accept invite** (route: `/invite/accept`).
2. Create your password and complete profile details if prompted.
3. Sign in at `/sign-in`.
4. Complete **two-factor authentication (2FA)** when asked. CAALM treats 2FA as part of protected access, not an optional garnish.
5. Land on your **role home dashboard** (CAALM picks the highest-priority role if you have more than one).
6. Open **View My Access** under Settings → confirm your roles and permissions look right. If not, stop and message your admin — do not invent workarounds.

## Path B — You are trying the demo

1. Go to [`/try`](/try).
2. Create or enter the demo sandbox session.
3. Explore with sample data. Demo mode skips some production constraints (including 2FA) so you can evaluate workflows quickly.
4. Use the guided tour tips when they appear — they point at real navigation anchors.

> [!WARNING]
> Demo is not your production org. Do not store real PHI/PII or assume integrations (like Outlook) behave the same. See [Demo vs production](/docs/troubleshooting/demo-vs-production).

## Your first useful action (pick one)

### If you manage agreements
1. Open **Contracts** or **Licenses**.
2. Find one item expiring soon.
3. Confirm the **owner**, **expiration date**, and supporting file are correct.
4. If something is wrong, fix it or open an approval / reassignment action.

### If you are an admin
1. Open **User Management**.
2. Invite one real teammate with the correct role + division.
3. Require 2FA in System Settings if your policy demands it.
4. Open Billing & Integrations only after users can sign in cleanly.

### If you are a viewer
1. Open your dashboard.
2. Identify the top risk or expiry widget.
3. Learn how to escalate (who owns the record) without editing.

### If you publish news
1. Open the Content Creator dashboard.
2. Draft one article.
3. Publish only when you have `news.publish`.

## Success checklist

- [ ] You can sign in twice in a row (session + 2FA stable)
- [ ] Your home dashboard matches your job
- [ ] You can find at least one contract or license you care about
- [ ] Notifications are not muted into oblivion
- [ ] You know who to ask when a nav item shows a lock
