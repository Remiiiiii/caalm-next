Day-one access is not adoption. Use this week plan to build the habits CAALM rewards.

## Day 1 — Orient

- Read [Mental model](/docs/concepts/mental-model) and [Permissions](/docs/concepts/permissions).
- Open **View My Access** and screenshot / note your permission set.
- Walk your role guide once end-to-end.

## Day 2 — Clean the queue you own

- Contracts/licenses: fix missing owners, bad dates, and unlabeled files.
- Calendar: add the renewals you already know are real.
- Notifications: enable the alerts you actually need; mute noise deliberately.

## Day 3 — Learn the approval path

- Open **Proposals & Approvals** for contracts and/or licenses.
- Watch one item move through review → approve (or ask a teammate to narrate).
- Confirm you understand who can approve versus who can only edit.

## Day 4 — Prove auditability

- Open **Audits → Compliance Status** and **Audit Logs**.
- Pick one agreement and confirm you can answer: what changed, who changed it, when.

## Day 5 — Leadership visibility

- Open Analytics (Overview / Quick View / C-Suite as permitted).
- Identify the single risk you would bring to a standup.
- If you are a manager, confirm your division analytics match reality.

## Weekend / buffer — Harden the org (admins)

- Invite remaining users with correct divisions.
- Turn on required 2FA if policy says so.
- Connect Outlook only after calendar owners are trained.
- Document your internal “who to ping” list for locks and missing permissions.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> CAALM value compounds when ownership is explicit. A perfect upload with no owner is still an operational risk.
