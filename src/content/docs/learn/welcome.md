CAALM Docs is the complete operating manual for CAALM Solutions — the compliance and agreement lifecycle platform your team uses to store, track, approve, and renew contracts, licenses, audits, and related work.

## Who this is for

- **New users** who need a safe path from invite → first useful action
- **Managers and executives** who need dashboards, risk signals, and renewal discipline
- **Admins** who invite people, assign permissions, and configure security
- **Content creators** who publish company news
- **IT staff** who operate the IT portal
- **Anyone stuck** who needs a troubleshooting path that does not shame them

## How the docs are organized

| Section | Use it when you want to… |
|---|---|
| Learn | Get oriented and productive quickly |
| Concepts | Understand *why* the product behaves the way it does |
| Guides by role | Follow a playbook for *your* job |
| Feature reference | Go deep on one product area |
| Admin playbooks | Stand up and govern an organization |
| Troubleshooting | Fix a specific failure mode |

## How to use these docs like a pro

1. Start with [What is CAALM?](/docs/learn/what-is-caalm) if you are brand new.
2. Do the [Quick start](/docs/learn/quick-start) once.
3. Read the [mental model](/docs/concepts/mental-model) — it pays for itself forever.
4. Open your [role guide](/docs/guides/super-admin).
5. Keep the [permissions catalog](/docs/reference/permissions-catalog) bookmarked.

> [!TIP]
> Prefer search (⌘K / Ctrl+K). Docs are written in the language users actually use: “I can’t see a contract,” “2FA locked out,” “who can approve?”

## What “good” looks like in CAALM

A healthy CAALM organization has:

- Clear **owners** for every critical agreement
- **Deadlines** that surface early enough to act
- **Approvals** that create accountability without bottlenecks
- **Permissions** that match real job duties (not tribal knowledge)
- An **audit trail** people trust during reviews

That is the outcome these docs are designed to help you reach.
