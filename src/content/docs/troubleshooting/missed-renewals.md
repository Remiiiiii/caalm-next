A missed renewal is usually a process failure with technical symptoms.

## Reconstruct the miss

1. What was the true expiration date?
2. What date was stored in CAALM?
3. Who owned the record?
4. Which notification channels should have fired?
5. Were those channels muted / misaddressed / unconfigured?
6. Did approvals stall so renewal work never started?

## Immediate repair

- Update the record with truthful status/evidence
- Assign an accountable owner
- Create calendar milestones for the recovery plan
- Notify stakeholders explicitly (do not rely on “they must have seen the bell”)

## Permanent repair

- Fix date quality at intake
- Test one synthetic upcoming expiry alert quarterly
- Ensure owners are humans who still work there
- Keep Notification Center hygiene part of weekly ops
