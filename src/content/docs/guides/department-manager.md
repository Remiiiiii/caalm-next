You are the frontline owner of operational compliance for your division.

## Home base

`/dashboard/departmentmanager` — action queue, compliance hero metrics, department contracts, monitoring widgets.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Your user record needs a correct **division**. If dashboards look empty, fix division before assuming the product is broken.

## Your daily loop

1. Open the action queue — what is overdue or due soon?
2. Confirm owners on anything critical.
3. Push reviews/approvals forward (or request them).
4. Update dates when real-world renewals happen.
5. Escalate blockers early (missing file, missing approver, vendor silence).

## What you usually can / cannot see

Managers are often scoped away from org-wide “All Contracts,” some advanced resources, and org billing/system settings. That keeps focus on *your* floor of the building.

If you need broader visibility for a temporary program, ask an admin for a deliberate permission change with an end date.

## Quality bar for “done”

A record is healthy when:

- File evidence exists
- Owner is current
- Expiration is real
- Status matches reality
- Next action is obvious

## Working with viewers and executives

- Viewers may monitor your area — treat questions as free QA
- Executives will ask risk questions from analytics — keep metadata trustworthy

## Monday triage template

1. Sort by expiration ascending
2. Anything < 30 days: confirm renewal owner + evidence plan same day
3. Anything < 90 days: schedule the work
4. Anything missing owner: assign before noon
5. Anything stuck in approval > 5 business days: ping approver with link

## Conversation starters with your team

- “What did we renew offline that never made it into CAALM?”
- “Which vendor packet is incomplete?”
- “Who is backup owner when you are out?”

Write answers into the system, not into memory.
