Company news is how leadership and ops communicate inside CAALM without burying updates in email.

## Home base

`/dashboard/content-creator` — article stats, list/editor, analytics tabs.

## Permissions that matter

- `news.create` — draft
- `news.update` — edit
- `news.publish` — make live
- `news.delete` — remove
- `news.read` — consume (most users)

## Publishing workflow

1. Draft in the article editor.
2. Write a title humans can scan on a dashboard widget.
3. Keep the first paragraph outcome-oriented (“What changed / What to do”).
4. Publish only when ready — drafts are fine; accidental publishes are not.
5. Check the company news feed as a normal user would.

## Editorial standards that pay off

- One idea per article
- Link to the CAALM record/workflow when asking people to act
- Avoid dumping policy PDFs without a summary
- Update or retire stale posts that create conflicting instructions

## Analytics

Use news analytics to see whether people open what you publish. If nobody reads renewal warnings, change channel/title/timing — do not just publish harder.
