The CAALM Assistant launcher provides AI-assisted workflows inside the product.

## Capability families

- `ai.chat` — conversational help
- `ai.document_analysis` — understand documents faster
- `ai.meeting_prep` — prepare for operational meetings
- `ai.image_generate` — image generation when enabled

## How to use it well

- Give the assistant the business question (“What expires in 45 days for residential?”) not just “help”
- Verify answers against source records before acting
- Do not paste secrets into prompts that do not belong in chat logs

## Governance

AI features are permission-gated like everything else. Admins should grant AI access deliberately — especially document analysis on sensitive agreements.
