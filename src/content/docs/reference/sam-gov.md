Advanced Resources (`/contracts/advanced-resources`) connects CAALM users to SAM.gov opportunity search.

## Who typically gets it

Org-wide commercial/compliance roles. Department Managers and Viewers often do not see this nav item by design.

## How to use it

1. Search opportunities relevant to your programs
2. Capture promising leads on [Funding and retention](/docs/reference/funding-retention) (Pursuits tab)
3. When an award becomes a contract, bring the executed agreement into CAALM’s contract lifecycle with owners and dates

## Do not confuse discovery with system-of-record

SAM results are opportunity intelligence. Executed agreements still need proper contract records, files, and monitoring inside CAALM.

Related: [Funding and retention](/docs/reference/funding-retention), [Contracts](/docs/reference/contracts).
