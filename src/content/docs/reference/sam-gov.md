Government Contracts (`/contracts/government-contracts`) is SAM.gov opportunity search inside CAALM.

## Who typically gets it

Org-wide commercial/compliance roles. Department Managers and Viewers often do not see this nav item by design.

## How to search

1. Open `/contracts/government-contracts`
2. Filter by keyword, procurement type, set-aside, state, organization, and response deadline
3. Run the search, then sort the table by title, organization, posted date, or due date
4. Clear chips one at a time when a filter is too tight
5. Save a search you will reuse (saved searches replay the same filters)

Open a notice for the SAM.gov detail. From there you can:

- **Generate proposal** — starts an [RFP proposal](/docs/reference/rfp-proposals) workspace (`proposals.create`)
- Capture the lead on [Funding and retention](/docs/reference/funding-retention) (Pursuits tab)

## After you win

When an award becomes a real agreement, bring the executed file into CAALM’s contract lifecycle with an owner and dates. SAM search is discovery. Contracts is the system of record.
