Quick Notes is the sticky-note widget on many home dashboards. Use it for short operational reminders that sit next to expiry and news widgets — not as a replacement for contracts, tickets, or formal records.

## Where you find it

- Dashboard **Quick Notes** card in the widget carousel (executive / admin and similar shells)
- Create with the sticky-note-plus control; open the full list with the go-to-page control on the same header

## Creating and editing

1. Open **New Note** from the widget
2. Add a title and body (rich text: bold, italic, lists, links)
3. Choose destination:
   - **Dashboard only** — stays in the widget list
   - **Pin to calendar** — also shows on the calendar month for a chosen day and time
4. Save

Hover a note in the widget to reveal **Edit** and **Delete** for that row only. The list preview truncates long bodies so the carousel stays readable; open edit or the all-notes dialog for the full text.

## Pin to calendar

When you pin a note:

- Pick a **date** from the calendar picker (years from 2026 onward in the year list)
- Pick a **time** with hour / minute / AM·PM columns
- For **today**, past times are muted and blocked — the time must be in the future
- For a **future day**, all times stay available

Pinned notes appear as note icons on the calendar **month** view for that day. Click the icon to open the day’s note list, then open a single note for the full body, edit, or delete.

### Calendar note dialogs

- **Notes for [day]** — time, title, short preview, and a chevron into the detail view
- **Single note** — full rich content, “Pinned to …” badge, back to all notes, and **Edit note** / **Delete** actions

Pinned notes are still your personal Quick Notes records; they are not the same as calendar **events** (meetings, invites, shared calendars).

## Widget carousel tip

Scroll inside the Quick Notes list to move between notes. Horizontal drag or chevrons move between widgets — vertical scrolling inside the notes list should not shove the whole carousel sideways.
