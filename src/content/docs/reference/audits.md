Audits in CAALM cover **posture**, **prep**, and **history**.

## Screens

- Audit Readiness: `/audits/readiness` — [full guide](/docs/reference/audit-readiness)
- Packet preview: `/audits/readiness/preview`
- Compliance Status: `/audits/status`
- Audit Logs: `/audits/audit`

## Audit readiness

Use **Audit readiness** when you want a customer-facing score and punch list from live contracts and licenses, scheduled snapshots, AI prep help, and a charted PDF packet.

Important: readiness is **CAALM data hygiene and prep**, not an official HRSA, DCF, funder, or CPA determination.

Read the step-by-step journey: [Audit readiness](/docs/reference/audit-readiness).

## Compliance status

This is where leadership inspects whether controls and risk indicators look healthy. Use it to spot systemic issues (chronic missing owners, repeated late renewals), not just single records.

## Audit logs

Activity history answers:

- Who changed what?
- When?
- In what context?

Permissions: `audit.view`, `audit.export`.

## How to prepare for an external review

1. Open [Audit readiness](/docs/reference/audit-readiness) and clear critical insights
2. Pick the agreements in scope
3. Confirm files + owners + approvals exist
4. Export the readiness packet and preserve logs as policy requires
5. Resolve obvious metadata lies before the meeting
6. Assign a single narrator who can click through CAALM live

> [!TIP]
> The best audit meeting is boring. Boring means the system already told the truth.
