Billing lives under Settings → Billing & Integrations and requires `settings.billing`.

## Operating practices

- Assign a primary billing owner and a backup
- Review usage meters before an upgrade decision
- Keep invoices somewhere finance already trusts
- Document why a plan change happened

## Before changing plans

1. Confirm who pays
2. Confirm what feature gates matter to your workflows
3. Confirm timing (avoid mid-renewal chaos if finance needs notice)
4. Confirm who will verify the change stuck

## Warning

If you cannot see billing, you lack permission. Request access — do not borrow credentials.
